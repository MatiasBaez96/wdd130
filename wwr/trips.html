Half Day Adventure

  • Class II–III
  • Ages 8+

The perfect first taste of white water. Float through gentle rapids, learn basic paddling skills from our guides, and stop at a quiet beach for a swim. Great for families and first-time rafters.

Canyon Run

  • Full day
  • Class III
  • Ages 12+

Paddle deep into red rock canyon walls on a full day of steady rapids. Includes a riverside lunch, a short hike to a hidden waterfall, and plenty of time to soak in the scenery.

  • Ages 14+

Take on our most famous rapid. This trip is built around big waves and nonstop action, with expert guides reading every line. Some previous rafting experience is recommended.

$99 per person

  • Class IV–V
  • Ages 16+

Our ultimate adventure. Two days of serious white water, a night of camping under the stars, and the legendary drop at Thunder Falls. All camping gear and meals are included.
