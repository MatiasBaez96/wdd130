History

Rafting began as a way for people to explore and navigate rivers long before it became the exciting adventure sport we know today. Early river explorers used simple wooden boats and rafts to travel through powerful currents, discover new territories, and transport people and supplies. Over time, these river journeys evolved into recreational adventures, attracting people who wanted to experience the excitement of rushing water and the beauty of nature..
