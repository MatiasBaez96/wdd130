History

Rafting began as a way for people to explore and navigate rivers long before it became the exciting adventure sport we know today. Early river explorers used simple wooden boats and rafts to travel through powerful currents. Over time, these journeys evolved into recreational adventures, and today rafting offers different levels of difficulty for both beginners and experienced rafters.
